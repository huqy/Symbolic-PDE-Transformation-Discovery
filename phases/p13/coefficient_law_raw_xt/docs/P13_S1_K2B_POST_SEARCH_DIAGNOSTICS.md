# P13-S1-K2B — Post-search diagnostics

K2B begins only after K2A has irreversibly frozen the response-blind S1 horizon and TRAIN membership. It is diagnostic evidence, not a candidate-selection stage.

It evaluates the complete frozen FULL clear cohort and the separately preserved FULL numerical-boundary-unresolved cohort. Every branch retains its S1 AST, theta, deterministic gauge, and fit provenance; there is no refit or branch reselection.

The diagnostic opening consists of:

1. zero-refit operator evaluation on the two preregistered fresh within-family diagnostic fields and the two existing cross-family diagnostic fields, at G33 and G65;
2. the preregistered coefficient-channel ablation: every AST-visible coefficient-jet channel is replaced by the pointwise six-TRAIN-field mean while the actual field remains in validity and pushforward/operator physics;
3. lower-order transformed-coefficient diagnostics on TRAIN and diagnostic fields;
4. response-blind hit-rate-versus-parent-J search-mechanism curves from the immutable proposal/branch ledgers;
5. carry-forward of the three K2A fairness axes without forming a scalar composite.

Diagnostic outcomes cannot change K2A membership, S2 eligibility, search budget, AST, theta, gauge, threshold, or branch identity. DEVELOPMENT, SEALED_FINAL, and all response outcomes remain unopened.

The private within-family payload is not copied into the active tree. K2B verifies the K0R commitment SHA and reads the committed tar.xz directly; only a commitment/opening receipt and diagnostic results are stored in the authoritative S1 run.

K2B PASS means the diagnostic protocol executed completely and provenance/data-boundary checks passed. It does not mean every candidate transferred successfully. Candidate-level invalidity or numerical sensitivity is retained as diagnostic evidence rather than converted to a stage failure or candidate filter.
