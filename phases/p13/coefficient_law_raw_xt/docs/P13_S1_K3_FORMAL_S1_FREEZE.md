# P13-S1-K3 — Formal S1 Freeze

K3 is a stage-freeze/adjudication step. It performs no new search, no candidate refit, no response solve, and opens neither DEVELOPMENT nor SEALED payloads.

It verifies and freezes:

- the complete 4-seed x 3-arm x 8192 first-rung registry/accounting;
- complete proposal/skeleton/branch/exact-equivalence provenance by path/SHA/bytes/line-count;
- the entire clear `OPERATOR_QUALIFIED_TRAIN` cohort as the S2 ACTIVE candidate cohort;
- numerical-boundary unresolved branches separately as REFERENCE lineage;
- K2B/K2C as REFERENCE mechanism evidence with zero membership authority;
- already committed DEVELOPMENT and SEALED payload metadata without opening them;
- identity and frozen S0 coefficient-blind NULL controls for future S2 response non-discrimination checks;
- a canonical S2-entry context and exact S2 active-input manifest.

No top-k, percentile, Pareto, weighted score, target survivor count, diagnostic-transfer rule, theory-alignment rule, or response-aware filter is permitted.

S2 reads candidates and coefficient commitments from authoritative paths in place. K3 creates no duplicate candidate/data archive.
